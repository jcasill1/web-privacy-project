Research References

The information presented on this website is based on educational resources from organizations that provide information about web technologies and online privacy.

These sources explain how cookies work, how online tracking affects internet users, and how people can protect their privacy.

1. MDN Web Docs

Topic: HTTP Cookies

MDN Web Docs provides information about web development technologies. Its guide explains how HTTP cookies work, how they are stored, and the differences between cookie types.

Website: MDN - Using HTTP Cookies

2. Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)

Topic: Online Tracking and Browser Fingerprinting

The Electronic Frontier Foundation is a nonprofit organization focused on digital rights. Its Cover Your Tracks project explains how websites and advertisers may identify browsers and track users.

Website: EFF - Cover Your Tracks

3. Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

Topic: Consumer Privacy and Online Tracking

The Federal Trade Commission is a United States government agency that provides information about consumer protection and privacy. This article explains how websites use cookies, tracking pixels, and browser fingerprinting to collect information about users. It also provides advice on reducing online tracking and protecting privacy.

Website: FTC - How Websites and Apps Collect and Use Your Information

4. Google Chrome Help

Topic: Managing Cookies and Browser Settings

Google Chrome Help provides instructions for managing cookies and changing browser privacy settings. These resources can help users understand how to control cookies stored in their browsers.

Website: Google Chrome - Manage Cookies
