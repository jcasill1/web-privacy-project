About This Project

The topic of my website is Cookies and Online Privacy. I chose this topic because cookies and online tracking are common parts of using the internet, but many people may not understand how they work.

My goal is to explain these topics in a simple way so visitors can better understand how websites collect information and what they can do to protect their privacy.
